Is It Really Free?

Yes. Here are the questions we hear most.

What does “free” mean?

There is no cost to your business to have a machine. We supply, install, stock and maintain it. The machine is supported by the snacks and drinks people choose to buy.

Is there a minimum number of people or foot traffic?

Every location is different. Get in touch and we’ll take a look at your space and let you know if it’s a good fit.

How do people pay?

Just tap a card, open the door, take what you want, and close it. The machine’s AI computer vision handles the rest.

Who restocks and fixes the machine?

We do. Regular stock checks and customized selections keep it full of what your people actually want, and if something isn’t working, contact us and we’ll take care of it.

Can we choose what goes in the machine?

Yes. Tell us what your team or guests like and we’ll tailor the selection, from classic snacks to energy drinks, coffee pods and more.
